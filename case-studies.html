RESEARCH NOTE · SEPTEMBER 2026

AI is changing the finance workflow.

What is changing?

Financial institutions and accounting organisations are increasingly applying AI to document processing, research, transaction analysis, fraud detection, reconciliation, forecasting and customer service. Central-bank and industry commentary also highlights the importance of understanding the specific use case, managing model risk and maintaining accountability.

What does it mean for accounting?

The most immediate effect is not simply replacing a task. AI can change where human attention is spent: routine extraction and matching can become more automated, while review, judgement, controls, exception handling and interpretation become more important.
